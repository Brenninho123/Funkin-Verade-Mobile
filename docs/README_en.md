# Funkin em Verade

![Funkin Verade Logo](/assets/shared/images/titlescreen/logo.png)

_If you missed out_, Funkin em Verade is a FNF mod about Mundo Torajo.

You're Blulfi, a youngster who loves singing rap and has Morana as his attractive and lovable girlfriend.

Together they go trough challenges against _airhead's team_ ('n other original characters from Verade) with fun and epic music, and with a captivating eagerful story! All for FNF and MDT fans.

## Install

To play the mod, simply download it [here](https://github.com/BernardoGP4504/Funkin-Verade/releases) or via [our gamebanana](https://gamebanana.com/mods/644716).

As for making mods, just follow the [build instructions](/docs/BUILDING_en.md).
